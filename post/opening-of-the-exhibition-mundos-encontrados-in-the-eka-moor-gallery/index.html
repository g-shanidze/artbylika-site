Opening of the exhibition ‘Mundos Encontrados’ in the Eka & Moor Gallery

2017-03-12

The artist Lika Shkhvatsabaia participates with three works

The exhibition ‘Found Worlds’ opened on Tuesday 7 March. It brings together a selection of works of different formats and genres developed by 10 national and international artists and where we will have the pleasure of enjoying the works of Lika Shkhvatsabaia who presents her wonderful pictorial proposal.

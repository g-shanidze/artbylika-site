Exhibitions & Events

Paisajes Secretos in Madrid

2025-05-14

Likas Shkhvatsabaia´s solo exhibition has opened with great success at the Cultural Center Emilia Pardo Bazán, located in the heart of Barrio de Salamanca in Madrid. The opening night featured an official presentation accompanied with Georgian wine, and a wonderful turnout of guests.

There are presented up to 30 medium and large format paintings.

The exhibition will be open to the public until June 1st.

Curator of the exhibition: Judith Cuba

Don’t miss the chance to visit!

The Secret Landscapes collection connects painting with emotions, explores the landscape linked to memory, touching on themes that speak to us of life, family memories, geographical landscapes, travels through foreign lands, intimate landscapes, literature and music, and the manifestation of desires and chimeras.

Those who visit the exhibition will enjoy a detailed tour as each creation is a journey into the unknown, a place to explore and discover. An evocative experience that will leave its mark on the viewer's memory.

Each work is a unique visual poetry, built with subtle images imbued with a silent mystery, narrated with the verses of universal poets of all times, among them Georgian authors that the artist has rescued to give voice to her painting.

Exposiciones y eventos

Paisajes Secretos

2024-11-04

Solo exhibition of Lika Shkhvatsabaia ¨Paisajes Secretos¨opens at Ducal Palace in Medinaceli, Soria on October 5 and will last until October 31, 2024.

It is orginized by DeArte Medinaceli Foundation and The Embassy of Georgia to Spain.

The curator of the exhibition: Judith Cuba.

The Secret Landscapes collection connects painting with emotions, explores the landscape linked to memory, touching on themes that speak to us of life, family memories, geographical landscapes, travels through foreign lands, intimate landscapes, literature and music, and the manifestation of desires and chimeras. Each work is a unique visual poetry, built with subtle images imbued with a silent mystery, narrated with the verses of universal poets of all times, among them Georgian authors that the artist has rescued to give voice to her painting.

Art deepens and brings light, reveals states of the soul where reality is sometimes hidden under an apparent truth, or hides among the nuances only perceptible through the artist's mediation.

The exhibition is composed of medium and large format works in which Lika Shkhvatsabaia presents her latest works and where she fuses traditional and mixed techniques channelled through abstract expressionism, figuration, colour and matter; a unique combination of contemporary aesthetics that transcends the conventional and constitutes a treat for the eyes.
